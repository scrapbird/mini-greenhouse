## Auckland Library of Tools (ALoT)

**Address:** Newton/Grey Lynn, Auckland. **Site:** https://www.aucklandlibraryoftools.com/

**Membership:**

| Tier | Regular | Concession (student/CSC/gold card) |
|---|---|---|
| Monthly | $45 | $30 |
| Half-yearly | $60 | $45 |
| Annual | $105 | $75 |

**How it works:**
- Up to **10 tools at a time**
- Default loan: **7 days** (4 days on high-demand items)
- Most tools free with membership; some small per-loan charge
- Battery tools: +$1/loan
- Volunteer-run, non-profit, since 2019
- Browse inventory: https://aucklandlibraryoftools.myturn.com/

**Recommended path for this build:**
1. **Half-yearly $60** covers build window with slack.
2. Pre-check inventory online (router + jig + circular saw + mitre saw + sander + clamps).
3. Plan ALoT trips in batches — 10 tools per loan, 7 days each.
